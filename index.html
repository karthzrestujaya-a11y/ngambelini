Privacy notice

Ngam Beli Ni (NBN) pilot · Last updated 10 October 2026 (rev. 2)

NBN is a price-comparison service run by Kaarthigeyen a/l Ramachandren in Malaysia. This notice explains, under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010, what we collect, why, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Your email address and invite code — to log you in and keep the pilot to invited members.
  • What you search (typed words, pasted links, scanned barcodes) — to show results and learn which products to add.
  • Buy taps — the product, price and time when you tap a Buy button, so we can match your order and pay your cash back.
  • Photos you choose to search with — sent once to Google's Gemini service to read the product name. We keep only what it reads (product name, and for shop screenshots the price, shop and pack size), never the photo. Prices you share this way may be used to update our price list.
  • Your DuitNow ID (only when you claim cash back) — to pay you.

What we do not collect

No IC number, no bank card, no Shopee or Lazada password, no contacts, no location.

Who else sees it

  • Shopee and Lazada receive a short random code with your Buy tap (not your name or email), so their reports can show which orders came through NBN.
  • Service providers that run the app for us: Supabase (database and login), Resend (login emails), Google (photo search), GitHub (website hosting). Some of them store data outside Malaysia.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for other companies' advertising.

How long we keep it

For as long as you are a member, and up to 7 years for cash-back payment records. Delete your account and we remove the rest within 30 days.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or stop using NBN at any time. Email hello@ngambelini.com and we will reply within 21 days.

Commission

We may earn a commission when you buy through our links. You never pay extra. 40% of what we earn on your confirmed orders comes back to you as cash back.
