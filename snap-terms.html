📸 Snap & Earn — pilot terms

Beta pilot · Last updated 18 October 2026 · Pilot terms may change or end at any time.

How it works

  • Snap a clear photo of a shelf price tag in a physical shop (supermarket, pharmacy, kedai runcit) using the Snap button in NBN.
  • Each week we pick a category (e.g. baby milk). Photos in that category earn points.
  • Each approved photo = 10 points. 1,000 points = RM1.
  • Clear photos are approved straight away. Unclear photos are checked by us within 48 hours.

Limits

  • Up to 30 photos a day and RM20 a month per member.
  • Real shop photos only — no screenshots, no photos from the internet, no repeated photos.
  • The pilot has a fixed total budget. When it is used up, Snap & Earn stops.

Getting paid

  • At the end of each month, you are paid if you have at least RM5 in points and made at least 1 Shopee order through NBN that month.
  • We pay by DuitNow to your phone number by the 14th of the next month (next working day if the 14th is a weekend or public holiday).
  • Less than RM5? It stays in your wallet and carries to next month.
  • You get an email receipt, and your wallet shows "Dibayar".

Fair use

We may reject photos or close accounts that break these rules (e.g. fake, copied or edited photos, or many accounts for one person). Our decision is final during the pilot.

Your photos

Photos are stored privately for 30 days so we can check them, then deleted. We keep the price details (item, price, shop, date) to show better prices in NBN. Please don't include people's faces. See our privacy notice.
